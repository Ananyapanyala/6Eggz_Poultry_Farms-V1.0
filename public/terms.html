Terms & order policy

Terms of use and order requests

Product listings depend on the information and availability configured by the business. Prices, stock and delivery coverage should be confirmed by the 6EGGZ team before an order is accepted.

Order requests

Submitting the cart form records a request for confirmation. It does not complete a purchase, reserve inventory, confirm delivery or process payment. The team should separately confirm the product, final price, delivery charges, delivery timeline and payment method.

Cancellation and refunds

No live payment or automated refund workflow is connected in this version. The business must publish a reviewed cancellation, refund, replacement and complaint-handling policy before collecting payments.

Food and nutrition information

Customers should follow applicable storage and handling instructions on the final product packaging. Research-stage nutrition concepts are not offered for sale. Any public nutrition claim must be supported by genuine evidence and reviewed for applicable labelling and regulatory requirements.

Availability and changes

Product status may change as stock and operating readiness are confirmed. Contact the team through the site for current information.
