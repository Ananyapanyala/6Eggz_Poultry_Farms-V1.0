Production approach in the DPR

Environment-controlled housing. Automated daily operations.

The project design describes an environment-controlled layer farm supported by automated feeding, drinking, egg collection, manure handling and ventilation systems. Veterinary support, flock-health monitoring, biosecurity and preventive disease management are part of the proposed operating framework.

Circular farm outputs

The DPR also plans revenue from farm by-products.

Alongside table eggs, the project financial model includes spent-layer bird sales and poultry manure sales. These are business-to-business outputs, not retail egg products.

🌱

Poultry manure

Proposed buyers include farmers and compost manufacturers for agricultural use, subject to handling and applicable requirements.

🐔

Spent-layer birds

Proposed sales channels include live-bird traders and poultry processors at the end of the production cycle.

📦

Commercial egg channels

Wholesale traders and distributors are primary buyers; institutions, retail networks and food-service buyers are secondary channels.

Future R&D · Not a current product

6eggz GlucoBalance is a concept, not a validated egg.

The separate R&D report proposes a nutrition-characterised egg concept and suggests 6eggz NutriBalance as a safer fallback name if “Gluco” implies an unapproved disease-related claim.

01

Expert formulation

Qualified poultry nutritionist to formulate and approve basal and trial diets. The report does not finalise additive dosages.

02

Controlled trial

Evaluate flock performance, egg quality and any proposed enrichment with a defined protocol.

03

Laboratory testing

Verify finished-egg nutrient composition, stability, sensory quality and measurement basis.

04

Regulatory review

Review product name, label and claims before any commercial launch. No diabetes treatment or blood-glucose-lowering claim is made.

ⓘ
Important: GlucoBalance/NutriBalance is not listed as available for sale. The R&D report explicitly says it is not a validated formula, medical product or approved commercial launch. The website will not invent a formula, nutrient level, lab result or therapeutic benefit.
