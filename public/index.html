6eggz Growing Years

Alternative working name: 6eggz Smart Nutrition Egg

Family-nutrition concept focused on measured and repeatable egg composition. Candidate targets include DHA/total omega-3, choline, iodine, B12, folate, iron, zinc and protein; each target needs a separate feasibility and safety review.

A process you can
hold us accountable to.

The R&D reports specify the following evidence process for the proposed Growing Years and GlucoBalance concepts. These are planned controls, not a claim that testing has already been completed.

01

Feed & flock care

Plan balanced feed with qualified poultry nutritionists, then monitor production and hen welfare.

02

Controlled trials

Validate proposed feed changes using a suitable, well-recorded trial design.

03

Independent testing

Measure the finished egg and document the lab, method, report date and measurement basis.

04

Review before claims

Assess consistency, shelf life, safety, labelling and applicable regulatory requirements.

Are nutrition-enriched eggs available now?

Enriched eggs are a proposed research direction, not an available product by default. They can be listed for sale only after nutrient content, safety, production feasibility, labelling and applicable regulatory requirements have been verified and approved.

Do eggs alone prevent or treat anaemia?

No. Anaemia can have multiple causes and needs appropriate assessment and care. Eggs can be one part of a varied diet, but they are not a standalone treatment for anaemia, stunting or vitamin deficiencies.

How are product prices and availability determined?

Only product details entered by the business team are shown. An order request is checked by the team before delivery and payment are confirmed.

Can my hotel, retailer or institution request regular supply?

Yes. Use the supply enquiry form and include your expected quantity, location and frequency. The enquiry is stored for follow-up and is not itself a quotation or supply agreement.

What does a verified nutrition claim mean here?

The relevant evidence and measurement basis must be recorded, reviewed and approved before the claim can appear publicly. An administrator setting a label is not a substitute for genuine laboratory and regulatory evidence.
