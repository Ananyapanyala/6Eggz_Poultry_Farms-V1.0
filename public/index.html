Future R&D · Not for commercial sale

Two specialist egg concepts. Evidence comes first.

These concepts are taken from the supplied R&D reports. Neither is an approved formula or a current product available for purchase.

01

6eggz Growing Years

Family-nutrition concept. Candidate measurements include DHA/omega-3, choline, iodine, B12, folate, iron, zinc and protein. These are research targets—not claimed nutrient values.

02

6eggz GlucoBalance

Proposed nutrient-characterised egg concept. Possible measurements include protein, carbohydrate, proximate composition and optional omega-3 profile if justified. 6eggz NutriBalance is the alternative name if required after review.

03

Controlled research

A qualified poultry nutritionist must review feed formulation and trial protocol. Baseline testing, controlled trials and confirmation batches must be completed and recorded.

04

Laboratory and claim review

Verify composition, safety, shelf stability and relevant accreditation scope; then review the final name, packaging and claims under applicable requirements.

ⓘ
Research status: Both concepts are marked “Research Concept — Not Approved for Commercial Sale.” No memory, intelligence, attention, glucose-lowering, diabetes-treatment or medication-replacement claims are made.
