Privacy

Privacy notice

This launch version stores details submitted through contact, supply-enquiry and order-request forms so the 6EGGZ team can respond and manage the request. Depending on the form, this may include name, contact details, organisation, quantity, delivery location, message and order items.

How information is used

Information is intended for enquiry follow-up, supply planning, order-request administration and related customer service. It should not be sold or used for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis and any required consent.

Access and retention

Administrative records are restricted to authorised project administrators. The business must set a retention schedule, establish a privacy contact and handle access, correction or deletion requests in accordance with applicable law before full commercial launch.

Payments

This site does not currently process online payments or store card data. An order request is not a payment confirmation. If a payment provider is added later, this notice should be updated to identify it and explain related processing.

Contact

Business contact details are not yet configured in this launch version. Please use the contact form on the main website to reach the team.

This is a draft operational notice, not legal advice. Have it reviewed and complete the business-specific details before launch.
